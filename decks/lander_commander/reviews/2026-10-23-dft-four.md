## Willowrush Verge — **ADD, cut Botanical Sanctum**

> {T}: Add {U}. {T}: Add {G}. Activate only if you control a Forest or an Island.

- **Best case:** always enters untapped, and taps for both colours whenever you
  control one of 26 basics (14 Forest, 12 Island).
- **Against Botanical Sanctum** ("enters tapped unless you control two or fewer other
  lands"): Sanctum is tapped from your 4th land on. A 44-land deck with Aesi, Azusa,
  Dryad, Burgeoning, Exploration and Oracle of Mul Daya reaches its 4th land around
  turn 3, so for most of a turn 9–12 game Sanctum is a tapland. Verge's only loss is a
  turn-1 Verge with no basic out, which makes U only.
- Land for land, no role or count changes. Low impact — the audit found the mana base
  is not a weakness — but the swap costs nothing.
- **Runner-up cut:** Dreamroot Cascade (tapped only on your first two land drops, so it
  is closer to Verge than Sanctum is).
- Price: $4.95 (fetched 2026-10-23 10:51 UTC). Owned.

## Nissa, Worldsoul Speaker — **ADD IF you don't take the audit's Aetherize-for-Ghalta swap; then cut Ghalta**

> Landfall — Whenever a land you control enters, you get {E}{E}.
> You may pay eight {E} rather than pay the mana cost for permanent spells you cast.

- **Best case:** the only card in the deck that turns landfall into casting spells
  (0 other energy or free-cast effects). Every land is a quarter of a free permanent;
  fetches count twice (5 sacrifice-fetch lands: Evolving Wilds, Terramorphic Expanse,
  Fabled Passage, Myriad Landscape, Blighted Woodland), and Ancient Greenwarden makes
  the trigger fire an extra time. Twelve permanents at MV 6+ to cast this way (Aesi,
  Greenwarden, Kodama of the East Tree, Rampaging Baloths, Avenger, Cultivator
  Colossus, Jin-Gitaxias, Koma, Nezahal, Craterhoof, Terastodon, Ghalta). Energy
  carries over between turns (ruling 2025-02-07). Fills the MV4 hole (2 → 3). Matches
  the deck's landfall preference.
- **Rate vs Lotus Cobra:** Cobra gives 1 mana per land, used straight away. Nissa gives
  1/4 of a permanent per land; for an MV8 spell that's worth 2 mana per land, and for
  anything MV4 or under it's worse than Cobra. So her value comes from the top end.
- **The attack — marginal impact:** she builds energy fastest exactly when Aesi and
  the extra land drops are already online, which is when the deck is already ahead.
  With 1 land a turn (Aesi dead, slow draw), she makes a free spell every four turns.
  She does nothing against any of the pod's four threats (combo, go-wide, mill,
  aristocrats). She's a 3/3 for 4, and any energy you've banked is useless once she
  dies (no other energy payoffs).
- **Disagreement check:** 1.2% of Aesi decks (194/16,129). The community mostly passes
  on her, and the marginal-impact problem above is a good enough reason. Nothing makes
  this deck special enough to override that.
- **Why ADD IF and not NO:** the cut has to be worse, and Ghalta is. The audit already
  called it the third finisher behind Craterhoof and Overwhelming Stampede (same
  reasoning here). Nissa does something nothing else in the deck does; Ghalta does what
  two other cards do. But the audit gave Ghalta's slot to **Aetherize**, the deck's only
  mass answer, and that addresses how the deck actually loses. Interaction beats engine
  for that slot.
- Not Goldvein Hydra: the audit gave that slot to Reality Shift (exile vs aristocrats),
  for the same reason.
- Price: $0.20 (fetched 2026-10-23 10:51 UTC). Owned.

## Sab-Sunen, Luxa Embodied — **NO**

> At the beginning of your first main phase, put a +1/+1 counter on Sab-Sunen. Then if
> it has an odd number of counters on it, draw two cards.
> Sab-Sunen can't attack or block unless it has an even number of counters on it.

- **Best case:** an indestructible draw engine that doesn't need lands, so it survives
  destroy-wipes that Aesi and Tatyova don't, and keeps drawing when land drops stall.
- **Rate:** 2 cards every other turn, starting the turn after you cast it, so about 1
  card a turn for 5 mana. Tatyova (same MV, same colours) draws 1 per land, and this
  deck plays 2+ lands a turn. It can only block on the round after an even-counter
  turn, so it's an unreliable wall against go-wide.
- **Redundancy:** the 5th repeatable draw engine in a deck whose commander is a draw
  engine. The audit also flagged that the deck can **deck itself** (Aesi + 6 extra
  land-drop effects + a mill player). More draw makes that worse.
- Survives being free: yes. Price isn't the reason.
- Price: $1.96 (fetched 2026-10-23 10:51 UTC). Owned.

## Agonasaur Rex — **NO**

> Cycling {2}{G}. When you cycle this card, put two +1/+1 counters on up to one target
> creature or Vehicle. It gains trample and indestructible until end of turn. [8/8 trample, MV5]

- **Best case:** never dead. Early it's a 3-mana instant that replaces itself and
  gives Aesi indestructible against a destroy effect; late it's an 8/8 trampler for 5.
- **As protection:** it covers destroy only, protects one creature, and needs 3 mana
  held up in a deck that taps out. Heroic Intervention does the job better for 2. The
  audit already turned down cleaner protection (Tyvar's Stand, Lightning Greaves), and
  this is worse than both. I'm not reversing that for a weaker card.
- **As a threat:** it's the weakest of 12 MV 6+ bodies, with no ETB, no landfall and no
  land text. Cycling doesn't trigger landfall either.
- The only realistic cut (Ghalta/Goldvein Hydra) is already claimed above, and Nissa
  makes better use of it.
- Survives being free: yes.
- Price: $0.91 (fetched 2026-10-23 10:51 UTC). Owned.
