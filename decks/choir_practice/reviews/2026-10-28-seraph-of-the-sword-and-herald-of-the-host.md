# Seraph of the Sword for Dazzling Angel; Herald of the Host for Angel of the Ruins

Date: 2026-10-28. Deck: choir_practice. These are the user's chosen swaps; this file reviews them. It follows `2026-10-28-firemane-herald-for-vitality-dazzling.md` and replaces the Firemane Commando plan in that file.

**Verdicts:**
- **Herald of the Host for Angel of the Ruins: ADD.**
- **Seraph of the Sword for Dazzling Angel: ADD (marginal).** It does the job the user is buying it for. Its limits are listed below.

## Verified text (Scryfall, fetched 2026-10-28 23:00 UTC)

- **Seraph of the Sword** {3}{W}, 3/3 Angel. Flying. "Prevent all combat damage that would be dealt to this creature." $0.35.
  - Ruling: a trampler blocked by Seraph must still assign lethal damage (3) to Seraph, and the rest can go to the player.
- **Herald of the Host** {3}{W}{W}, 4/4 Angel. Flying, vigilance, myriad. $0.39.
  - Rulings: the tokens "enter the battlefield attacking" and "enter at the same time". "enters with" abilities still apply to them. Copies don't copy counters.
- **Angel of the Ruins** {5}{W}{W}, 5/7 artifact creature Angel. Flying. On entering, it exiles up to two target artifacts and/or enchantments. Plainscycling {2}. $0.23.

## Herald of the Host: ADD

In a 4-player game, every attack with Herald makes two token copies, one attacking each other opponent. Each token is an Angel entering under your control, so in one combat:

- **Giada:** each token "enters with an additional +1/+1 counter … for each Angel you already control." With Giada, Herald and two more Angels out, each token is a 4/4 with 4 counters, so 8/8 fliers.
  - Uncertain: the two tokens enter at the same time, so I assume they don't count each other. There is no official ruling on this. If they did count each other, the tokens would be bigger, not smaller.
- **Cathars' Crusade** triggers twice, putting a counter on your whole team each time.
- **Bishop of Wings** triggers twice, for 8 life (10 with Angel of Vitality).
- **Righteous Valkyrie** gains life equal to each token's toughness, and **Seraph Sanctuary** triggers twice.
- **Payoffs:** each of those gains is a separate event for Archangel of Thune, Lyra, Archangel of Dawn, Exemplar of Light and Dawn of Hope. The turn's total easily clears Resplendent Angel's 5, Valkyrie Harbinger's 4 and Ob Nixilis's end-step check.

Unlike Herald of War, this card multiplies the deck's main engine every turn it attacks, and it is a 5-drop replacing a 7-drop. Vigilance means Herald is still home to block after attacking, which matters for a player who gets targeted.

Limits:
- The tokens are exiled at end of combat. Exile is not dying, so Bishop of Wings makes no Spirits from them.
- Herald has to survive to attack. It is a 4/4 for 5 that will draw removal.
- Herald's own myriad needs at least two opponents, which is always true in this pod.

**What leaves with Angel of the Ruins.** Austere Command is gone (cut for Kindred Judgment, 2026-10-24), so this cut thins artifact removal noticeably:

| Answer type | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Targeted artifact removal | Generous Gift, Grasp of Fate, Angel of the Ruins | **Generous Gift, Grasp of Fate (2)** |
| Targeted enchantment removal | Get Lost, Generous Gift, Grasp of Fate, Angel of the Ruins | 3 |
| Mass artifact/enchantment removal | Farewell | Farewell |

You also lose plainscycling. The 2026-10-14 Herald of Eternal Dawn review kept Angel of the Ruins for exactly these two reasons: it fixes an early draw that is short on lands, and it is a two-for-one artifact answer. Both reasons still hold. Herald of the Host outweighs them, but **the deck is now at its minimum for artifact answers**. Any future cut of Generous Gift or Grasp of Fate should be weighed against that.

## Seraph of the Sword: ADD (marginal)

- **The user's case holds.** Seraph can block one non-trampling attacker of any size, every turn, and survive.
  - Deathtouch doesn't kill it either, because the combat damage is prevented.
  - It doesn't depend on opponents choosing to cooperate, which was Firemane's weakness.
- **Limits:**
  - It stops only one attacker per combat.
  - Trample gets most of the damage through, because only 3 has to be assigned to Seraph.
  - It does nothing against removal, burn or wraths.
- **Cost to the engine:** Dazzling Angel was the deck's only gain source that triggers on non-Angel creatures entering. That means the Servos, Spirits and Soldiers stop feeding Thune and Lyra, Archangel of Dawn. Dazzling was already the softer of the two original cuts, so this is acceptable.

## Aggregate delta

- **Curve:** a 3-drop becomes a 4-drop, and a 7-drop becomes a 5-drop. Net −1 total mana value, and the top end shrinks.
- **Angel count:** unchanged.
- **Lifegain engine:** −1 per-enter source (Dazzling Angel) and +1 repeating burst of Angels entering (Herald of the Host). Net stronger.
- **Interaction:** −1 artifact/enchantment answer, which leaves artifact removal at its minimum.

## Addendum: the user cuts Angel of Vitality instead of Dazzling Angel

The user chose to keep Dazzling Angel and cut Angel of Vitality for Seraph of the Sword. **This is a reasonable call, and Herald of the Host is the reason it now holds up.**

- **Thune and Lyra count events, not amounts.** Archangel of Thune, Lyra, Archangel of Dawn, Exemplar of Light and Dawn of Hope all trigger on *each* lifegain event. Angel of Vitality makes gains bigger but adds no events. Dazzling Angel adds an event for every creature entering. Each Herald of the Host attack now brings in two creatures, which is two more events, so two more rounds of counters on the team from Thune.
- **Vitality's best trick matters less now.** Its key line was Bishop of Wings' 4 life becoming 5 to turn on Resplendent Angel. On a Herald turn, Bishop already gains 8.
- **What you give up:** Vitality is a 4/4 flier for 3 whenever you're at 25 or more life, and Dazzling is a 2/3. Vitality also pushed toward Righteous Valkyrie's +7 and Angel of Destiny's +15. That's a real loss of body and reach, but it doesn't flip the call.

Before Herald of the Host, Vitality was the stronger of the two. With Herald in the deck, Dazzling is at least even with it.
