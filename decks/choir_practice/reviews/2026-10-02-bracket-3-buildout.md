### Two modes and the bridge

1. **Go tall in the air.** Giada puts counters on each Angel that enters. That stacks with the anthems: Lyra Dawnbringer, Thraben Watcher, Always Watching, the three Banners, Angelic Field Marshal and Metallic Mimic.
2. **Lifegain.** The deck already gains a lot of life incidentally, but almost nothing converts it into board. Today's only payoffs are Exemplar of Light, Righteous Valkyrie's +2/+2 at 47 life, Angel of Vitality's +2/+2, and Speaker of the Heavens (needs 47 life).

**The missing bridge** is cards that turn incidental lifegain into Angels or counters. Those cards serve both modes, so they are the strongest adds here.

### Magus of the Moat: verdict moved to ADD

Only four of the deck's own creatures lack flying (Bishop of Wings, Starnheim Aspirant, Metallic Mimic and Angel of Invention's Servos), and none of them is meant to attack. All the deck's Spirit and Angel tokens fly.

### Ghostly Prison: ADD, cut Firemane Commando

The user asked whether the deck should get cheap protection like the Edgar deck (see `markov_chains` review 2026-09-14-pillowfort-deterrence on branch `claude/mtg-card-expert-markov-3xizsf`).

Ghostly Prison `{2}{W}`: "Creatures can't attack you unless their controller pays {2} for each creature they control that's attacking you." Price $5.87 (fetched 2026-10-02 14:52 UTC); cheapest print is The List CHK-10 at $5.68. Giada EDHREC: 20.2% (7,274 decks).

**Case, in this deck specifically:**
- The deck already has two attack deterrents, and **both are creatures**. Magus of the Moat stops non-fliers attacking anyone. Archangel of Tithes taxes {1} per attacker while untapped. A single creature wipe removes both, and that is exactly when this deck is most open to the pod's ground attacks. Ghostly Prison is an enchantment, so it survives creature wipes and covers the rebuild turns.
- While Magus is up, Prison covers what Magus doesn't: **flying** attackers.
- It is one-sided. The deck's own attacks are untouched.
- Pod fact (user, 2026-10-02): most wins come from ground attacks. That makes deterrence directly against the pod's main way of winning.

**Redundancy check** (effect × frequency × duration): Magus is a total ground lockout on a fragile 0/3. Tithes is a {1} tax that needs the Angel untapped. Prison is a {2} tax on every attacker, wipe-proof. Same family, but they cover different gaps, and Prison is the only one that survives the deck's worst moment.

**Alternatives rejected:**
- Windborn Muse ($0.80) has the same text but is a creature, so it dies in the same wipe as the other two.
- Norn's Annex ($5.61) costs 5.
- Crawlspace ($8.75) caps attackers at two. That is worse than a {2}-per-creature tax unless the pod goes very wide with ramp to pay through it.

**Cut: Firemane Commando.** "Whenever another player attacks with two or more creatures, they draw a card if none of those creatures attacked you." Once Prison and Magus push attacks onto other players, this clause pays opponents a card for doing what the deck is making them do. Its own draw clause (you attack with 2+) is replaceable: Minas Tirith fires on the same condition, alongside Folk Hero, Herald's Horn, Tome of Legends, Endless Atlas, Vanquisher's Banner, Exemplar of Light, Wojek Investigator and Inspiring Overseer.

Runner-up cut: Commander's Sphere, since ramp is 12 pieces deep. Choose it instead if you'd rather keep Angel density; Firemane is an Angel. Angels after this change: **26**. Changes from base: 24.

### Clever Concealment replaces Teferi's Protection (cut stays Exorcise)

The user asked whether Clever Concealment should take Teferi's Protection's slot. Yes. This matches the Edgar verdict (`markov_chains/reviews/2026-08-31-teferis-protection.md`), and the reasons hold in this deck too.

Clever Concealment `{2}{W}{W}` Instant, convoke: "Any number of target nonland permanents you control phase out." Price $3.67 (fetched 2026-10-02 14:53 UTC). Not a Game Changer.

**The slot was opened for one gap:** Flawless Maneuver, Akroma's Will and Avacyn all rely on indestructible, so exile wipes and -X/-X effects got through. Phasing beats both, so **Clever Concealment covers the same gap.**

Where it is better here:
- **Lands stay in.** Teferi's phases out everything, lands included, so you can't cast Path, Generous Gift, Get Lost or Akroma's Will until your next turn.
- **Convoke is cheap in this deck.** The creatures are mostly vigilant: Giada; Always Watching and Thraben Watcher for nontoken creatures; Angelic Field Marshal for everything while Giada is out. They are usually untapped on opponents' turns to pay for it.
- **It is selective.** Ghostly Prison can stay in play during a creature wipe and keep taxing attackers. Teferi's phases Prison out along with everything else.
- **Teferi's turns off the lifegain engine for a turn cycle.** Your life total can't change and every payoff is phased out. Clever Concealment can leave chosen pieces in.
- **No Game Changer used.** The deck is back to 0.

What Teferi's does that Concealment doesn't: it protects **you** from lethal damage, life loss and targeted effects for a turn cycle. In this pod, most wins come from ground attacks, and Magus of the Moat and Ghostly Prison already handle those. That is the narrower risk.

**Would Teferi's win at $0?** No. The lands-up, selectivity and lifegain-engine points are not about price.
