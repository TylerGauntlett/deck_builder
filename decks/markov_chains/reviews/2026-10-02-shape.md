## Keystone
- **Primary action:** cast Vampire spells → Edgar's eminence makes a 1/1 Vampire
  each time, *from the command zone* ("if Edgar is in the command zone or on the
  battlefield"). 36 Vampire creatures fuel it. Go wide.
- **Capitalization:** two lanes off the same board.
  (a) *Combat* — anthems (Legion Lieutenant, Captivating, Stromkirk, Charmed Groom,
  Lord of Lineage), counters (Cordial, Indulgent, Edgar attacking), Shared Animosity.
  (b) *Aristocrats* — sac outlets turn the wide board into death triggers.
- **Conversion to a win:** combat alpha (Akroma's Will: double strike + flying);
  drain stack (Blood Artist, Cruel Celebrant, Bastion, Vein Ripper, Mirkwood Bats,
  Sanctum Seeker), doubled by Bloodletter; and the late-game two-card loops
  **Vito + Bloodthirsty Conqueror** / **Marauding Blight-Priest + Bloodthirsty
  Conqueror** (Commander Spellbook: 2 combos assembled — permitted by meta.json,
  untutored). The drain lane wins without the combat lane — that's resilience.
- **Nonbos found:**
  - **Vampire Nocturnus** — "As long as the top card of your library is black".
    Only **43 of 99** library cards are black (34 lands are colourless), so it is
    off more often than on — and it reveals your top card the whole game.
  - **Bloodletter of Aclazotz** — "during your turn" only. Grave Pact / Blood
    Artist triggers on opponents' turns (removal, wraths) are not doubled. Minor.
  - **Teferi's Protection** — "your life total can't change" switches off Vito,
    Blight-Priest and Conqueror for a cycle. Known, accepted (08-31 review).
  - **Edgar, Ancient Bloodlord** (new) is *not* a nonbo: "you gain 1 life" per
    death feeds Vito / Blight-Priest, so it replaces Zulaport's drain only when one
    of those is out. Alone it's a 2-mana sac outlet + lifegain — a step down from
    Zulaport as a standalone payoff, a step up as an outlet.

## Commander role → target shape
Edgar Markov is a **generator** (eminence, always available) and a secondary
**amplifier** (attack counters — only once cast for 6). So: **diamond** — few
dedicated generators, many amplifiers, few payoffs.
Plan adjustments: the deck is loud and attacks, and the pod runs long → keep
protection healthy; the only standing structural hole (08-31 audit) is board
recovery after a wrath.

Shape reading: generators are right-sized (every Vampire is already eminence

more cards that convert *deaths and Vampire count* into cards.
