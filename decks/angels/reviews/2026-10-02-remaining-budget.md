Budget before this review: $232.68 of ~$300 spent, so ~$67 left.

## Where the deck still has room

Protection, removal, ramp and counter engines are all deep after the earlier passes. The remaining gaps:
1. **No one-sided board answer to go-wide ground decks.** That is how this pod mostly wins. Farewell, Austere Command and Sunblast Angel all hit your own board too, unless Clever Concealment or vigilance line up.
2. **Card draw is spread across many small sources** (Folk Hero, Herald's Horn, Archivist, Tome, Endless Atlas, Vanquisher's Banner, Exemplar, Wojek, Inspiring Overseer, Minas Tirith, War Room, Bonders'). None of them turns the deck's lifegain into cards.
3. **The weakest remaining slots**: Emeria Shepherd (its recursion overlaps Emeria, the Sky Ruin), Invoke the Divine (the deck now has about 7 other artifact/enchantment answers), and Endless Atlas.

## ADD — 3 cards, $19.94

| Add | Cut | Price | Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite** | Emeria Shepherd | $15.57 (SLD #209; $20.81 default) | "Other creatures you control get +2/+2. Creatures your opponents control get -2/-2." A **one-sided wipe** of every opposing creature with toughness 2 or less, and a permanent shrink on the rest. That answers the go-wide ground decks this pod wins with. It is also the biggest anthem in the deck: it turns every Servo, Spirit and Samurai token into a real attacker. Its cost is 7 with only Pearl Medallion reducing it, since it isn't an Angel. Emeria Shepherd's job (recursion) is already covered by Emeria, the Sky Ruin. |
| **The Wandering Emperor** | Invoke the Divine | $3.69 | Flash planeswalker. When cast on an opponent's turn, she can use her abilities at instant speed: −2 exiles a **tapped** creature (an attacker, in a ground pod) and gains 2 life, which triggers Thune and Lyra AoD. +1 is a counter plus first strike; −1 makes a 2/2 token, which triggers Cathars' Crusade and Dazzling Angel. Proliferate (Metastatic Evangel, Norn's Choirmaster) adds loyalty. Invoke the Divine is the least flexible of about 7 artifact/enchantment answers (Farewell, Generous Gift, Get Lost, Grasp of Fate, Austere Command, Angel of the Ruins, Eiganjo for creatures). |
| **Dawn of Hope** | Endless Atlas | $0.68 | "Whenever you gain life, you may pay {2}. If you do, draw a card." There is no cap per turn. It is the first card that turns the deck's ~14 lifegain sources into **cards**, and the deck gains life many times a turn (Bishop of Wings, Righteous Valkyrie, Dazzling Angel, lifelinkers, Seraph Sanctuary). Late game, {3}{W} makes a 1/1 lifelink Soldier, which triggers Crusade, Thune and Lyra AoD. Endless Atlas does the same {2}-for-a-card job, but only once a turn. Giada EDHREC 15.1% (5,444 decks). |

## ADD IF — Teferi's Protection ($36.26, MAR #51), cut Valorous Stance

Still the right Game Changer #3 if you lose to combos, life loss or alpha strikes rather than board wipes. It fits the budget after the three adds above (about $5.88 left). Its cut moves from Invoke the Divine (now taken by the Emperor) to **Valorous Stance**: single-creature protection that Clever Concealment and Flawless Maneuver cover, plus a narrow removal mode.
