# Land Tax (2026-10-29): ADD, cut Mind Stone

Deck: choir_practice (Giada, Font of Hope), bracket 3, $40/card cap, ~$300 total. Evaluated against `base.txt` (100 cards).
Prices: Scryfall, fetched 2026-10-29 23:11 UTC. EDHREC: Land Tax card page. Spellbook: `combos.py --add`. Deck counts come from `cards.json` (generated 2026-10-28).

## Facts (verified this session)

**Land Tax** {W}, Enchantment. "At the beginning of your upkeep, if an opponent controls more lands than you, you may search your library for up to three basic land cards, reveal them, put them into your hand, then shuffle." Legal, colour identity W, **not** a Game Changer.
Ruling: the "if" is an intervening-if clause. The ability won't trigger unless an opponent has more lands than you, and it does nothing if that's no longer true when it resolves.

## Why this differs from the 2026-10-02 NO

The build-out review rejected it in one line: "Land Tax is unnecessary with 38 lands." Two things have changed since then:
- The deck now runs **37 lands**.
- **Angel of the Ruins was cut** on 2026-10-28 for Herald of the Host. The 2026-10-14 review had spared Ruins specifically because "its plainscycling covers exactly the bad early draw Herald adds." That cover is gone. The only land-smoothing card left is Secluded Steppe (cycling). Nothing in the 99 searches for lands (`cards.json`: the only "search your library" text is Path to Exile, and that search is the *opponent's*).

## Steel-man, then the tests

**Best case, as a claim that could be false.** It's a 1-mana engine that guarantees land drops and thins basics out of the library, in a deck with **seven** spells at mana value 7 or more and **five** WWW costs. It is now the deck's only answer to a land-light draw.

**Scope: whose lands, and how often does it trigger?** The condition is **any** opponent having more lands than you, checked at your upkeep. You play your land after upkeep. So from seats 2–4, every opponent who acted before you this round is a land ahead whenever they hit their drop, and it triggers with no sandbagging at all. From seat 1 it needs a ramping opponent or a missed land drop. **Path to Exile** turns it on directly: the opponent fetches a basic and goes up a land.

**What it supports (verified via `cards.json` and `cards.md`).**
- **The top end:** Avacyn 8; Elesh Norn, Emeria's Call, Herald of Eternal Dawn, Kindred Judgment, Ob Nixilis and Sephara 7. Three of these (Elesh Norn, Emeria's Call, Kindred Judgment) are **not Angels**, so Giada's mana, Aspirant, Incubator and Horn don't reduce them. Only real mana pays for them.
- **WWW costs:** Archangel of Tithes, Avacyn, Emeria's Call, Herald of Eternal Dawn, Sephara. Land Tax fetches **Plains**. The deck has 4 colourless lands (Bonders' Enclave, Radiant Fountain, Seraph Sanctuary, War Room) plus Three Tree City, and 3 colourless rocks (Sol Ring, Mind Stone, and Pearl Medallion as a reducer).
- **Plains-matters:** Emeria, the Sky Ruin ("if you control seven or more Plains" → return a creature card every upkeep) gets there turns sooner. Castle Ardenvale enters untapped if you control a Plains.

**Redundancy.** No other card in the deck does this job. Secluded Steppe's cycling is a one-shot, one-card version. The rocks accelerate mana, but they don't make land drops or fix WWW (Arcane Signet and Patchwork Banner do fix colour).

**Rate × frequency × duration.** Three basics per trigger, potentially every upkeep, for the whole game. Each trigger also strips three Plains out of the library, so later draws are more likely to be spells. With 37 lands that's the main value: it **cuts flooding** as well as screw. The shape review flagged flooding as a risk to watch.

**Marginal impact.** It helps in games you otherwise lose: hands with two lands and Giada, a stall on 4 lands with 7-drops in hand, or WWW cards stuck behind colourless sources. It doesn't win games. It makes the deck's draws more consistent.

**Worst-case draw.**
- Seat 1 against a pod with no ramp: dead until someone gets ahead. Sandbagging a land drop turns it on, but it slows your curve.
- Late topdeck: often a fresh trigger (opponents with ramp are ahead by then), otherwise a 1-mana blank.
- It's the cheapest card in the deck, so a dead copy costs very little.

**Anti-synergy.** None in the list. There's no landfall and no land-count payoff it switches off. The deck's mana rocks don't count as lands, so they don't stop it triggering.

**Bracket.** Not a Game Changer. It tutors basics only, so it doesn't feed any win line. `combos.py --add`: 0 combos, 0 near.

**Disagreement check.** 43.3% of Giada decks run it (16,826 / 38,846). The community says yes. The earlier NO named no deck-specific reason, and the reason it did give no longer holds. Independent reason: seven 7+ drops (three with no Angel discount), five WWW costs, and no other land smoothing since Angel of the Ruins left.

**Speed premise.** The verdict doesn't rest on it.

## The cut: Mind Stone

It's in the same role (mana), and the shape review called mana **over-supplied** at 9 rocks/reducers plus Giada. Mind Stone is the weakest remaining mana piece here:
- It makes **colourless** mana, which is the deck's pip problem, not its solution.
- Its late-game mode ("{1}, {T}, Sacrifice: Draw a card") is a single card. Land Tax gives better card quality across the game.
- What you lose: the turn-2 rock that lets you cast a 4-drop on turn 3. Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Giada's Angel mana and four reducers still cover early acceleration.

After the swap: lands 37, rocks/reducers 8 + Giada + Three Tree City, average nonland MV about 3.6, unchanged within rounding (a 2-drop out, a 1-drop in).

Runners-up, spared:
1. **Radiant Fountain.** The shape review's flood cut, "only if goldfishing shows flooding." 36 lands + Land Tax is a common build, but the rubric says to cut a spell for a spell. Take this cut instead if you'd rather keep the rock count.
2. **Patchwork Banner.** A rock that also fixes colour and gives Angels +1/+1. Better than Mind Stone on both counts.
3. **Valorous Stance.** Still reserved as the cut for Teferi's Protection.
4. **Fateful Absence.** Reserved by the Banishing Light review as the cut for Darksteel Mutation.
5. **Seraph of the Sword.** You chose it on 2026-10-28. Not reopened here.

## Condition to reverse

If you're usually seat 1, or the pod has no ramp decks and it sits dead in most games, put Mind Stone back.
