# Bruna, the Fading Light for Angel of the Ruins — NO

2026-10-02. Proposed by the user: "more popular on EDHREC and pairs with Gisela, the Broken Blade."
Evaluated against `complete.txt` (the proposed deck), all 72 non-basic cards' text fetched live this session.

This is the third look at Bruna today. The build-out review and the shape follow-up both said NO. This proposal is
different because it names the cut. A like-for-like 7-drop swap removes the earlier "the top end is already six cards
at 7–8" objection, so the card was re-evaluated on its merits rather than waved through on the earlier verdicts.

## Verified text

- **Bruna, the Fading Light** {5}{W}{W}, Legendary Creature — Angel Horror, 5/7. "When you cast this spell, you may
  return target Angel or Human creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield." Flying, vigilance. Melds with Gisela.
- **Angel of the Ruins** {5}{W}{W}, Artifact Creature — Angel, 5/7. Flying. "When this creature enters, exile up to two
  target artifacts and/or enchantments." Plainscycling {2}.
- **Gisela, the Broken Blade** {2}{W}{W}, 4/3 flying, first strike, lifelink. "At the beginning of your end step, if you
  both own and control Gisela and a creature named Bruna, the Fading Light, exile them, then meld them into Brisela."
  Not a "may", so the meld is forced.
- **Brisela, Voice of Nightmares**: Legendary Creature — Eldrazi Angel, 9/10. Flying, first strike, vigilance, lifelink.
  "Your opponents can't cast spells with mana value 3 or less."
- **Giada**: "Each other Angel you control enters with an additional +1/+1 counter on it for each Angel you already control."

## Steel-man for Bruna

- The bodies are identical (7 mana, 5/7 flier), so the swap costs nothing on curve or pips.
- The cast trigger is a 2-for-1 that advances the deck's actual plan. Two Angels enter, and Bruna takes Giada's counter
  for the one it brought back. Legal targets in the deck: 23 other Angels and 5 Humans (Bishop of Wings, Drannith
  Magistrate, Metastatic Evangel, Starnheim Aspirant, Magus of the Moat). These include Avacyn, Archangel of Thune,
  both Lyras and Sephara.
- It fills a thin category. The deck has **one** creature-recursion card, Emeria, the Sky Ruin (upkeep only, needs
  seven Plains, and it's a single land).
- Vigilance, plus one more legend to discount Eiganjo's channel.
- **Corrections to the shape review's meld paragraph.** Brisela is an Angel and enters the battlefield, so with Giada
  out it gets Giada's counters back. "You lose every counter on both" was overstated. Brisela's static also stops
  opponents from casting removal and counterspells at mana value 3 or less, which protects it. Both points favour Bruna.

## Attack

1. **Neither of the stated reasons carries the case.**
   - *EDHREC popularity* is not evidence of fit. In this deck it is also distorted by the meld: 98.4% of Gisela-commander
     decks run Bruna (929/944).
   - *The Gisela pairing* needs two specific cards on the battlefield at your end step. The deck has no tutors: the
     only library-search effects are Angel of the Ruins' plainscycling and Herald's Horn's top-card reveal. Brisela is a
     real upside, but a rare one, so it is a bonus and cannot be the reason.
2. **This swap reverses two cuts the deck made earlier today.**
   - *Invoke the Divine* was cut (remaining-budget review) because Angel of the Ruins stays as an artifact/enchantment answer.
   - *Emeria Shepherd* was cut because its recursion overlapped Emeria, the Sky Ruin.

   This swap does the opposite of both: it removes an artifact answer and adds recursion. Reversing both judgments needs
   new evidence, such as game results. Popularity is not new evidence.
3. **What leaves with Angel of the Ruins.** Counts are grouped by what each card can actually hit:

   | Category | Now | After swap |
   |---|---|---|
   | Targeted **artifact** removal | 3: Generous Gift, Grasp of Fate, Angel of the Ruins | **2** |
   | Targeted **enchantment** removal | 4: Get Lost, Generous Gift, Grasp of Fate, Angel of the Ruins | 3 |
   | Mass artifact/enchantment | 2: Farewell, Austere Command (both symmetric; the artifact modes also kill your own rocks and Banners) | 2 |
   | Creature recursion | 1: Emeria, the Sky Ruin | 2 |

   Get Lost does not hit artifacts. In unknown pods (10+ decks rotating, wins mostly through ground attacks), a single
   Equipment or a key artifact would have only two clean answers left in the deck. Recursion and artifact removal are
   equally thin. That makes this a sideways trade, not an upgrade.
4. **Plainscycling** keeps a 7-drop from being a dead card early. With 37 lands that matters less, and the shape review
   flagged flood as the risk. This is a small point in Bruna's favour.

**Net:** Bruna vs Angel of the Ruins is close, and closer than the two earlier NOs suggested. It is not a demonstrated
upgrade. Under default-NO, a close sideways trade stays NO.

## What would change this

Game evidence of either kind:
- Angels piling up in the graveyard in long games (the revisit condition set in the shape review).
- Angel of the Ruins' trigger regularly hitting nothing worth exiling, or the deck never being short of artifact answers.

Either one makes this swap an ADD as written. Adding another cheap targeted artifact answer elsewhere would also
remove most of the cost.
